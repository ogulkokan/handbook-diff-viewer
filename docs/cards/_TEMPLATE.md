# Card 000: title

One or two sentences: why this card exists.

## In scope

- What this card builds.

## Out of scope

- What this card does not build, and which card does.

## Acceptance

Each criterion names the test or the check that proves it.

- **A1** The result that a reader can see. Proof: the name of the test, or the browser check.

## Open decisions

Block on these. Do not invent an answer. Write the question, the options, the cost of each and a recommendation.

- None known.

## Handoff

Fill this in when the card is done.

- **Gate:** one row for each step: PASS, FAIL or NOT RUN.
- **Review:** the verdict, and each finding that was fixed.
- **Measured:** what a command or a browser check showed.
- **Not measured:** what nobody checked.
- **Left:** what the next card must know.
